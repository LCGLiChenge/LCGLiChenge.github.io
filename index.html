01 · Profile

About

I am an undergraduate student in Robotics Engineering at Zhejiang University, enrolled in 2024. My research interests sit at the intersection of visual representation learning and generative modeling. I am particularly curious about how compact, expressive visual tokens can support both high-fidelity reconstruction and image generation, with potential connections to embodied intelligence and robotics.

02 · Focus

Research Interests

I am interested in learning expressive visual representations and using them to build capable generative systems. My current focus is visual tokenization for image reconstruction and generation; in the longer term, I hope to explore how these ideas can contribute to perception and learning in robotics.
